Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Ataraktos collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network requests of any kind. There is no server to send anything to.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen sources, translation, values and kairos times.
  • Any words you write yourself, and any words you save.
  • Which words you have already seen, and whether you tapped Steady or Drifting.

All of it is written to a single file inside the app's own private storage on your iPhone. It is never transmitted. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Explanations

When you ask Ataraktos to explain a passage, the explanation is written by Apple Intelligence on your device. The passage is not sent to Anthropic, to the developer, or to any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally by iOS at the times you pick. No push server is involved. You can decline or turn them off at any time without affecting anything else.

Children

Ataraktos is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
